\section{Research Experience}
\entry{Ph.D. Student --- Department of Computer Science, University of Chicago}{Chicago, IL}
\subentry{Reinforcement fine-tuning for LLM reasoning; multi-agent LLM serving}{Mar. 2026 -- Present}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=4pt]
    \item Designed Question-begets-Question (QbQ), a scalable data-generation procedure in which a teacher model transforms competition-math problems into diverse variants probing the same underlying skills, enabling reinforcement fine-tuning from problem statements and final answers alone --- no oracle reasoning traces [1].
    \item Proposed a self-evolving curriculum that re-seeds QbQ each round from problems the current checkpoint can mostly solve; under an identical data budget this breaks the static-training ceiling, lifting Qwen2.5-Math-7B from 5.6\% to 16.5\% pass@1 on AIME with no saturation after 20 rounds [1].
    \item Introduced a unified typed-graph representation that casts multi-agent LLM systems as analyzable serving workloads, and developed static runtime patterns and dynamic accuracy--efficiency strategies achieving up to a 5$\times$ speedup while using 54--89\% fewer tokens on real GPUs --- while showing that decode dominance means prefill-only wins do not survive end to end, making phase-aware algorithm--system co-design essential [2].
\end{itemize}

\entry{Research Assistant --- Laboratory for Emerging Intelligence, UC San Diego}{La Jolla, CA}
\subentry{Advisors: Prof.\ Leon Bergen and Prof.\ Ramamohan Paturi}{Apr. 2024 -- Mar. 2026}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=4pt]
    \item Co-developed CT Open, an open-access, uncontaminated, live platform for the open challenge of clinical trial outcome prediction, enabling rigorous evaluation of LLMs on prospective (post-training-cutoff) trials [4].
    \item Contributed to DeepImagine, which teaches LLMs biomedical reasoning by training them to infer how observed clinical-trial results change under controlled perturbations (dosage, outcome measures, study arms), combining supervised fine-tuning on strict counterfactual pairs with RL from verifiable rewards [3].
    \item Re-architected a Mamba-2 State-Space Model with a classification head, creating a subquadratic model that preserves global context for efficient sentence retrieval in documents up to 256k tokens, and designed a link-based synthetic data pipeline exploiting natural long-range dependencies for long-context QA training [5].
    \item Constructed a fine-grained sentence-retrieval benchmark from 41 existing QA datasets via an LLM-assisted annotation-validation pipeline [5].
    \item Developed the RoBBR benchmark for risk-of-bias assessment in biomedical research from expert judgments on 500+ studies, designing subtasks that disentangle the retrieval and reasoning abilities of language models and building a human-validated GPT-4 annotation pipeline for ground truth [6].
\end{itemize}
